Kunstlabore

inspiring educators and artists to work together

STORY

make more art at school!

Art and creative thinking are fundamental learning blocks of a child’s education. Nonetheless integrating the artistic method to schools is a complicated task. For three years, five art groups (fine arts, literature, music, dance & theater) worked with schools to develop formats and methods to create qualitative art at school.

“Kunstlabore” is the online platform documenting their work, made to inspire educators and artists to work together.
